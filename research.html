Research

My primary research interests lie in Commutative Algebra, Combinatorics, and Algebraic Geometry, with particular emphasis on the interactions among these areas. My research focuses on free resolutions and homological invariants of modules and ideals, the connections between algebraic and combinatorial structures, and the behaviour of invariants under transformations of simplicial complexes and other combinatorial objects. I also maintain an independent interest in Graph Theory, including graph polynomials, graph invariants, and their connections with algebraic structures.

Research Interests

  • Polynomial Algebra
  • Combinatorics
  • Algebraic combinatorics
  • Algebraic geometry
