Mentoring and Supervision

Mentoring students and supporting their development as independent mathematicians has been an important part of my academic career. My thesis supervision at Government College University Lahore spans undergraduate, master’s, and doctoral research in algebra, combinatorics, graph theory, simplicial complexes, and related areas.
