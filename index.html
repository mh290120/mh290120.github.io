Welcome

I am currently pursuing my second PhD in Mathematics at Dalhousie University, where my research focuses on free resolutions of monomial ideals and their powers. I hold an Associate Professor of Mathematics appointment at Government College University Lahore (on approved study leave since August 2023) and have more than 15 years of experience in university teaching, mathematical research, graduate supervision, and academic leadership. I was also a Visiting Postdoctoral Fellow and Adjunct Assistant Professor at Tulane University (2019–2020).

My primary research interests lie in Commutative Algebra, Combinatorics, and Algebraic Geometry, particularly their interactions. I am interested in constructing free resolutions and studying algebraic and homological invariants through algebraic, combinatorial, and topological methods. My research also explores the connections among monomial ideals, graphs, and simplicial complexes, including how invariants change under transformations of combinatorial structures. In addition, I maintain an independent interest in Graph Theory, particularly graph polynomials and structural invariants.

Beyond research and teaching, I serve as Co-Manager of the Nova Scotia Math Circles Program, where I help organize mathematics outreach activities and promote mathematical curiosity among school students across Nova Scotia. I am also actively involved in initiatives supporting innovative mathematics teaching and professional development. My work reflects a strong commitment to advancing mathematical research, fostering student learning, and promoting mathematics through outreach and community engagement.
